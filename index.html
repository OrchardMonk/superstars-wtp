Definitions and where the numbers come from
Written
Business written in the month, as on the WTP tab's W rows.
Banked
Fees paid in the month, as on the WTP tab's B rows.
WTP
Written to paid: banked ÷ written.
Like for like
When the latest month is only part way through, comparisons with the prior month and with 2025 scale that month to the same share of the month.
Projection
Year to date + (year to date ÷ months active) × months left in the year, as on the WTP tab. Months active run from January for the team and from an adviser's first month on the tab (a £0 month counts). A month built from weekly reports counts as the share of the month elapsed, so months left includes the rest of that month.
